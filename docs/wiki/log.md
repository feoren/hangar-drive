## [2026-11-26] lint | ADR 0001: lasting pin cap and restore after a burst
Closed two gaps from the second acceptance review in pages/decisions/0001-foundation.md, section 5. The pin cap is now a lasting invariant: bytes that only admitted pins retain stay within `Q + H`, checked in every garbage collection step; pins are admitted in creation order, a pin that does not fit lapses for good (first pins win), and four retention classes (live, window, pin, minimum count) make each byte count against at most one cap. A pin restore allowance of `Q + H` per W days exempts revived bytes from pinned trees from the upload budgets, so a restore after ransomware does not wait for the budget the harmful device used up; the flow "burst, remove the device, reset the whole folder" is played through and needs no quota exemption. Disk bound recomputed to `3 × (Q + H) + B × (W + 1) + C` (`35 × Q + 3 × H` with the defaults), with the daily garbage pin attack replayed. Operator warning, review list and next steps follow. pages/syntheses/ux-concept.md: 9.4 step 3 says the reset normally does not wait for the daily budget. Conclusions are marked.
